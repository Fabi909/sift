How Signal Works

Signal isn't sentiment, and it isn't a price prediction. It's a mechanical check on whether a coin's trading volume and news coverage actually back up what its price is doing — built from two independent checks, combined into one of three statuses. No hidden inputs, no manual overrides.

The Volume Check

24-hour trading volume is compared to market cap. Thin volume is easy to fake or coordinate — volume that's a real share of market cap is much harder to manufacture.

Under 2% — thin 2–8% — borderline 8%+ — strong

The News Check

Coin name and symbol are matched against ~15 crypto news RSS sources, refreshed every 5 minutes. Multiple outlets covering a coin counts as stronger evidence than one article — and no match is stated plainly, never hidden.

The Three Statuses

Validated Volume clears 8% AND news matches.
Mixed Volume clears 8% with no news, or news matches with volume in the 2–8% zone.
Unvalidated Volume is under the 2% thin-trading floor.

Signal Confidence (0–100)

How strongly a coin clears its status — never changes which status it gets, just how convincingly. Weighted 65% volume strength, 35% news corroboration (3+ sources ≈ full marks). Shown in the Signal tooltip and each coin's detail page.

Signal Track Record

Each coin's Signal status is snapshotted, then later compared to its price now across 3/7/14/30-day windows. If Validated calls don't outperform Unvalidated ones over time, the badge isn't earning its keep.

Source Reliability

The same accountability idea, grouped by news source instead of status. Tracks each source's calls and the average price change since, across the same windows. Needs 3+ tracked calls before a source is ranked. Toggle "By Source" on the Track Record card.

What Signal Isn't

Sift is a research tool, not financial advice — it doesn't touch your wallet or execute trades. Validated means the evidence backs up real activity, not that price will keep moving. Always do your own research.
